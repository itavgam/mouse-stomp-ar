Mouse Stomp AR

Hold your phone so the camera sees the floor and your feet. Step on the mice with your real foot. Every mouse you squash is replaced by a bigger, faster one.

Don't stomp the yellow buddy, and don't let a mouse bite your leg. You have 3 lives.

Needs camera and motion access, and must be opened over https. Works best in good light, with shoes that stand out from the floor, and a steady phone.
